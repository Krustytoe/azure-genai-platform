# Remote Work Policy

Owner: People Operations.

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require director approval.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours whenever possible.

## Home office

New remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair or monitor. Internet service is reimbursed up to $50 per month.

## Working abroad

Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year requires approval from People Operations and Legal, because of tax and data-residency obligations.
