# Paid Time Off Policy

Owner: People Operations.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, which is 20 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines requests within three business days.

## Holidays

Larkspur Systems observes 11 paid company holidays each year, published in the HR portal every December.
