# Information Security Standard

Owner: Security Engineering.

## Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company system, including email, VPN and the cloud consoles. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and must auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 14 days of release.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer personal data and payment information, must never be pasted into unapproved AI tools or personal accounts.

## Reporting

Report suspected phishing or security incidents to security@larkspur.example or through the #security-help channel within one hour of discovery.
