# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Finance. Applies to all Larkspur Systems employees and contractors traveling on company business.

## Booking

Book all flights, hotels and rental cars through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Trips must be approved by your manager before booking.

## Meals and per diem

The domestic meal per diem is $75 per day. The international meal per diem is $110 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client entertainment event.

## Lodging

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $250 per night in standard-cost cities and $350 per night in high-cost cities such as New York, San Francisco, London and Tokyo.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip end date. Itemized receipts are required for any single expense of $25 or more. Reports submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.
